# Portfolio

This context describes the public record of Matthieu d'Oultremont's professional work and personal practice. It keeps production content distinct from material still being developed.

## Language

**Published record**:
A record shown to visitors. It is concise, accurate, and complete enough to stand on its own, even when a longer account will follow.
_Avoid_: Placeholder, draft

**Unpublished record**:
A record retained in the repository but excluded from the visitor-facing portfolio. It may be incomplete while being prepared.
_Avoid_: Published record

**Experience record**:
A published record of a professional role. Its dates and concise summary are sufficient for a visitor to understand the role without a longer case study.
_Avoid_: Case study, placeholder

**Experimental page**:
A route used to explore personal material before it is part of the public portfolio promise. It may be intentionally unlisted and excluded from search indexing.
_Avoid_: Public page, finished page
